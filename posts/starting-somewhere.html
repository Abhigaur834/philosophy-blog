Starting Somewhere

August 30, 2026

Every blog starts with some version of the same confession: I've meant to write this for a while, and I'm still not sure I have anything new to say. I'm starting anyway.

What you'll find here isn't a syllabus. It's closer to a notebook — essays on the ideas that keep resurfacing when I'm doing something else entirely: waiting for a call to connect, reading something unrelated, or just staring at a ceiling. Some of it will be about classical philosophical questions. Some of it will be about whatever is actually bothering me that week, examined a little too closely.

If you're here, thanks for reading. More soon.
