About the notebook

Curiosity before certainty.

I write here about the questions I can't quite put down — the ones that appear in ordinary moments and refuse to resolve neatly.

This isn't meant to be a syllabus or a collection of final answers. It is closer to a notebook: part philosophy, part observation, part thinking out loud. Some essays may lean toward classical questions; others may start with something surprisingly ordinary.

The goal is simple: slow down, look again, and make room for a better question.

Stay curious

If an idea here resonates with you — or you strongly disagree — that's worth something. Explore the project on GitHub and follow along as the notebook grows.
