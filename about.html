WHY THIS EXISTS

This isn't a syllabus or a collection of final answers. It is closer to a notebook: part philosophy, part observation, part thinking out loud.

Some essays begin with love or loss. Others begin with work, ambition, routine, identity, or the quiet feeling that there must be more to life than going through the motions.

The point isn't to sound certain. The point is to slow down, look again, and make room for a better question.
