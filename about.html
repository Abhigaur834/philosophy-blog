About the person behind the notebook

Curiosity before certainty.

I spend my days working with numbers, people, targets, and the uncertainty that exists between them.

My professional journey began with sales — learning how to listen, persuade, handle rejection, and keep moving when things did not go as planned. With time, that journey led me into operations and team management, where I learned a different lesson: behind every number is a person, and behind every result is a story.

Managing people has taught me that life, much like work, rarely moves in straight lines. Some days demand discipline. Some demand patience. And some simply ask us to begin again.

I have always been more interested in the questions beneath the obvious ones.

What makes a life meaningful?
Why do we remain attached to things that hurt us?
How much of who we are is really ours?
And when does ambition become something other than ambition?

These questions are what brought me to Just Unorthodox Philosophy.

This is not a place where I pretend to have figured life out. Quite the opposite.

It is a space to pause, observe, question, and write about the things that are easier to feel than to explain.

Some thoughts come from work. Some from relationships. Some from silence. And some from simply being human.

I don't write because I have answers.
I write because some questions deserve to stay with us a little longer.

— Abhishek GaurSales & Operations · Team Builder · Perpetual Questioner
